Manifesting While Broke

— S, founder of I Like It Deep

I see all these people on Reddit making money from blogging and selling digital products, and here I am struggling to do the exact same thing with my own site. I'm sorry, but I'm not about to pay for some class to teach me — most of what you can buy online for this stuff is a scam. Navigating this digital world is honestly kind of insane. I just want one of my side hustles, one of these websites, to actually take off and make money.

I got an offer to move into a commercial building and start my grooming business back up, but that's not until next year. It gives me time to save, but in the meantime it's frustrating. There's a million "do this, do that" tips floating around online, but without funds to actually start any of them, it's hard to get anything moving.

I'm trying to manifest, and paying people on Etsy to do spells for me at the same time, because why not cover all my bases. My time is coming — I know it. I know I'm capable. I grew up spoiled with material things and never had to think about money, so I never really learned how to budget or save. Now, without a real job and just focusing on this website, growth is slow. Getting people to even find my site in the first place is the hard part.

I post regularly on Pinterest and often on Reddit, and the results are still slow going. I know it'll take off eventually — I've got no shortage of ideas, the problem is always actually making them happen. I need to lay them all out and work through them one at a time instead of jumping around, and stay motivated and focused on a future where I'm actually comfortable.

Money doesn't make people happy, I know that. But it would be nice to have things of my own. Renting isn't always fun, and I'd love a real house with my boyfriend one day. We both have spending problems, if I'm being honest — if I really want something, I find a way to get it. So for now I'll just keep trusting the universe to make it happen. I know I'm worthy of all the possibilities and love this world has for me. I know I'll be successful and happy doing what I love again.
