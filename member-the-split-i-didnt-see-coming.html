The Split I Didn't See Coming

— S, founder of I Like It Deep

This week is sort of a big deal — I was referred to a DBT class. Tomorrow I go in for a three-hour interview to see if I'm a good fit. After checking every box for Borderline Personality Disorder, I want to do what it takes to manage my emotions. It's crazy because I can feel that crazy brewing inside me. I know what I'm thinking isn't always true, yet I'm stuck on stupid trying to make it true. It's not fun.

I just went through an episode last weekend. I have contact with my current boyfriend daily. There are times he needs a break, and I know I have to give him space after I've snapped. But this time was different. I saw him earlier that day, and afterward he was doing a side job — I was expecting to hear from him once he was done, like usual. As the night went on, I didn't hear anything. I sent messages, called — no response. I couldn't sit at home by myself, so I went out to the bar with a girlfriend to take my mind off it. It irritated me the entire night. I was expecting some response, but I didn't hear from him until late the next morning.

That was unacceptable to me — the lack of communication. Right away my mind went to the worst possible idea, and I was livid. He didn't understand it, but I made sure it was obvious how much it hurt me. Inside, I wanted him to hurt like he hurt me. I cried endless tears throughout the day with him, including trying to be as standoffish and short with him as I could. I finally voiced things like, "Do you even understand where I'm coming from and why I'm crying?" He said he got it — it was because he didn't respond, and he didn't think it was that big of a deal. Looking back, I was over the top about it, when I simply could have used my words to communicate everything. I'm hoping he won't do the same thing again, or I'll feel really stupid for letting it go.

Unfortunately, sex was able to "cure" my issues. I've heard people on TikTok talk about how sex can make you feel closer to someone and get confused with love — I completely agree. I've always been super sexual, and in my mind, sex cures everything. With this man, he reminds me that "sex isn't the most important thing in our relationship" when I'm in the mood and he isn't. It drives me bonkers, but sex is important to me — I want it like four times a day. I think sometimes he punishes me by not letting me get on it, lol. But if I feel like I'm in outer space thanks to that man, I'm obsessed, and he best never try breaking up with me.

Back to my original excitement — I hopefully will be starting this DBT class to help manage my heightened emotions. I'm very aware I go above and beyond and only see black and white during my "splits," as people on TikTok call it. I'd like to not be so heated and over-the-top emotional, and possibly not stress out my boyfriend so much. I'm beyond thankful he handles me and tolerates my behavior. I love him, and with my BPD, he is my "favorite person." The times he tries to pull away, I lose my shit — and I mean lose it. What they write about people with BPD, I hit that level. It's embarrassing after the fact. I feel so regretful and ashamed I let myself hit such a low.

I guess I'll keep you updated on how it goes, and my journey to better myself.
