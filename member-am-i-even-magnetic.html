Am I Even Magnetic?

— S, founder of I Like It Deep

Blah. That's the feeling when you try your best to show someone you're excited to see them, and you're met with an outcome that's the total opposite of what you imagined. Why do our brains fool us into thinking "oh, this is exactly how it'll go," when in reality it's nothing close? Why do we do this to ourselves, just to end up disappointed? Such a silly thing to do.

I mean, some outcomes turn out better than expected too. Maybe if we walked into every situation without expecting anything, it'd just be a fun surprise every time — living in the moment. That sounds a little crazy though, when you think about how we all tend to look back and dread the future instead.

I'd have to remind my ten-second brain to "hey, stay in the moment right meow" constantly, and that alone sounds exhausting. Or explain to me having ADD, and never really being able to concentrate on anything unless it's something you actually want to do, or have a crazy passion for. I'm just going off my own experiences here, trying to figure my life out. Better yet, trying to find myself and figure her out from the inside out.

I make my own life hard sometimes, choosing between options A, B, or C — usually picking whatever's exciting, fun, thrilling, instead of calm, normal, or whatever society tells you to do. I mean, for fuck's sake, do we ever truly get to be ourselves in a world that wants us all to fall in line? Do we take the polite mask off and actually say what we feel? Would that even benefit us, or just cause more turmoil?

I know for a fact I still wear my mask around certain people. It's exhausting. Most of the time I say what I actually feel, and sometimes I'm too honest, which ends up kicking me in the ass later. That crazy dopamine hit I crave from anything, or anyone, just to feel something — it does a great job of messing up my life.

We only get this one life before we leave this world and start fresh on the next. So in this life, do we sit and waste time scrolling on our phones when we could be outside enjoying nature? Do we keep doing things that hurt us, over and over? Do we actually learn the lesson each time, or do we ignore the truth and do whatever we selfishly want instead?

Do we do what actually makes us happy, without worrying about the opinions of people stuck in the same loop? Do we ever break free from society and become the "best" version of ourselves? Who the fuck are we? What makes us special? Are we even special enough to be loved by people?

Who knows. All I know is it's late, and I need to get to bed before I keep rambling on about nothing, when my eyes want to shut and my body's telling me to sink into bed, naked, with no distractions. That's my cue to listen and head to bed — leaving all of this for you to question too. Enjoy.
