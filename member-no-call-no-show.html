No Call, No Show

— S, founder of I Like It Deep

I admit I was rude. I did a no call, no show today. The thing is, I was totally ready to go! I was up early doing my makeup — and then I just accidentally canceled my ride to bring me there. Didn't call or anything. No communication at all. I just really didn't feel like doing a group event. Plus the bubbly energy from the instructor gets to be too much sometimes. I know she means well and is just doing her job — I just am a bitch sometimes.

There's a part of me that doesn't want to continue in this program, and another part of me that is dying to figure out ways to help myself when I'm in these moods. They're taking over my life and not making things easy — they love to self-sabotage and push people away. I hate it. I know I need to change things and accept this help, but I'm lazy, and a part of me is scared, or just not ready to hear things. In the moment of a build-up, I feel it happening, and I highly doubt much can truly help.

Even though DBT was created for people with borderline, I shouldn't give up this easy. It's hard because I want to do this to help improve my relationships with people — but then again, this is a huge commitment, and when I have someone making comments about how I'm not doing anything in life, it isn't the best to hear.

As much as this person gets the brunt of my episodes, you'd think he'd be supportive of me being in this. To some extent he is — but also, me sitting around picking my nose the other days of the week when I could have a job… it's frustrating. I'm lazy, okay. I've gotten used to doing nothing. Sure, I invested time into building a site, but because it's not making money, it doesn't count in his eyes. He's got zero faith in anything I do.

If he cared, wouldn't he look at my updates and tell me what looks good or not? No — he doesn't truly care, because it's not bringing in anything. Maybe it's a blessing he isn't looking. He'd be a little upset about my blog post putting our relationship on blast, but it's my blog, and if you don't like it, too bad.

I just want life to be easier. I do feel like I'm stuck and not going anywhere — and yes, it's because I'm lazy, and because now this damn anxiety creeps in about trying new things too. It's gross. I hate feeling this way. I had motivation years ago, always wanting to be something. Now I could care less. I just stopped giving a complete fuck about impressing anyone. Like, how do they affect my daily life in any way, to judge or look at my life? It's kind of a bitter, mean-spirited way to be — but this funk is just sticking around till I nip it in the butt.
