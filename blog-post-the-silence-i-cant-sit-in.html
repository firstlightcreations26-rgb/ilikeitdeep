The Silence I Can't Sit In

— S, founder of I Like It Deep

This dang thing... called self-awareness. It's such a tricky thing I'm trying to understand. If I'm aware of how I feel in the moment, I should be able to question the thoughts I have and remind myself it's just a thought. It's not real unless I make it real, or there's actual proof. Without proof, it's just an anxiety punch to make me go crazy. If I can look at my thoughts as passing words and let them pass by, instead of staying stagnant, maybe I can free myself from the cluster of anxiety that comes from these moments. It's a lot to wrap my mind around.

I wish life was easier. I could stop clenching my jaw 24/7, maybe relax my shoulders, and not be such a rigid bitch. Just a thought. I feel tension all over my body, and I'm aware it's unhealthy. Stress in general is unhealthy. It's hard to let my mind not believe everything it's saying. I'm going to attempt, daily, to catch myself before I spiral. I swear I can't even count on my hands how many times a day I let my thoughts run my life. Is shock therapy the only way out?...
