Tell Me I'm Not the Only One Who Does This

— S, founder of I Like It Deep

Tell me I'm not the only female who does this. Tonight my boyfriend handed me his phone while he went inside, so I could keep playing the game he was playing. It took everything in my nosy little brain not to go into his messages and check if he's talking to other girls. I don't trust any guy, if I'm being honest.

If I'd had the energy to pick a fight after seeing something, maybe I would have — but honestly, I don't even want to know if he's talking to anyone. I don't want to go to bed sad. I just want to keep doing what we're doing, and if something comes up, we deal with it then.

Would I personally enjoy him ever going through my phone? Probably not. It's not like I'm trying to get with anyone else, but I'm sure even my conversations with male friends wouldn't thrill him. If I saw other girls' names in his messages, my mind would assume the worst — and it could just be a friend. I know there's this whole thing about how guys and girls can't really "just be friends," and on some level, I can agree with that.

I'm open about any male friends I do interact with, too. I'd rather he hear it from me than find out some other way. I think that's one of the differences between us — he just doesn't think certain things are relevant to mention if they don't mean anything to him, but I'd much rather know if girls are trying to get with him, or flirting. It's sort of a respect thing. What happens if we're out somewhere and run into that person, and she's flirty with him in front of me, or says something only the two of them would understand, and I'm just sitting there looking dumb because he didn't think it was important enough to mention? Things like that get to me.

I care about this human, and after three years, I really hope he's loyal. Things were a little rocky in the beginning, but now I hope things are exactly as I feel they are. He's been a great friend, a support, and a loving person to me when I needed it most. I take his love for granted sometimes. I'd feel really sad if he ever left my life. I'm used to him always being there. I honestly can't imagine my life without him in it. I'm not sure if he feels exactly the same, but I hope so.

He just walked into my life right when I needed it. I'm so thankful things happened the way they did. We were friends before we ever became a couple — I never expected he'd end up being my boyfriend. I always thought he was way out of my league. He's so handsome, and his body is honestly so yummy. I love how muscular and sexy he is. He has these big arms that wrap around me when I sleep next to him, and I feel so safe. I fall asleep in his arms so fast every time. He's my peace. Where my heart feels safe. I love him so much. If only I could pick his brain sometimes, lol.

He's such a huge part of my life, and such a blessing. So — next time he hands me his phone, I'll do the same thing: not be a nosy little shit-starter, and just respect his privacy.
